The Shadow of the Wind

by Carlos Ruiz Zafón • ★★★★★

A love letter to literature and the city of Barcelona. This book is a labyrinth of mysteries and emotions that reminds us why we read in the first place. The "Cemetery of Forgotten Books" is an image that stays with you long after the final page.

Stoner

by John Williams • ★★★★☆

A quietly devastating novel about an ordinary life. It's a masterclass in stability and focused storytelling. Proof that you don't need explosions or grand adventures to write a deeply moving story.
